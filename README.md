# Lakshmi Priya Gopalsamy — Papers, Research & Writing

Hi, I'm Priya — an engineering leader and IEEE Senior Member working at the intersection of AI systems engineering, platform architecture, and engineering management. This repository is the home for my white papers, peer-reviewed research, and technical/practitioner writing: drafts, published versions, abstracts, and links to where each piece lives publicly.

Ms. Gopalsamy is an individual of extraordinary ability in enterprise platform engineering and technology leadership — a highly specialized field requiring mastery of distributed systems architecture, large-scale data infrastructure, and AI-driven operations, as well as the organizational expertise to lead and scale high-performing engineering teams in Fortune 500 enterprise environments.

I write about applied AI, agentic systems, AI governance and strategy, and software engineering practice — usually moving between the academic and practitioner worlds rather than staying in one lane. A single idea often shows up here in more than one form: a research paper, a conference talk, and a shorter practitioner article.

## Repository structure

```
.
├── papers/    Peer-reviewed papers, research commentaries, and academic manuscripts
├── blogs/     Practitioner articles and technical blog posts
├── talks/     Conference/meetup talks — abstracts, slide outlines, scripts
├── assets/    Shared images, diagrams, and figures referenced across pieces
├── LICENSE
└── README.md  You are here
```

Each paper, blog post, or talk gets its own subfolder named `venue-or-platform-short-title/`, containing at minimum a `README.md` with the abstract/summary, status, and links. Source files (`.md`, `.docx`, `.pdf`, slide decks) live alongside it.

### `papers/`
Academic and white papers — including manuscripts under review, preprints, and published versions. See [papers/README.md](papers/README.md) for the index.

**A note on manuscripts under double-blind review:** full text for anything currently under double-blind review is kept out of this public repo (or in a private branch/fork) until review concludes, to preserve reviewer anonymity requirements. Only title, abstract, venue, and status are listed publicly in the meantime.

### `blogs/`
Practitioner and technical writing published on Dev.to, Medium, Stack Overflow Blog, etc. See [blogs/README.md](blogs/README.md).

### `talks/`
Conference and meetup talks — abstract, outline, and (once delivered) slides or recordings. See [talks/README.md](talks/README.md).

## Status legend
